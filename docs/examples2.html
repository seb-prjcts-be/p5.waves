p5.waves Examples 2

Beyond oscilloscopes — waves as behaviour, rhythm, and character. Each example shows a different domain where wave formulas inject life into composition, material, motion, and mood.

concept: feeling → formula · palette · speed · density

Mood Machine

Choose a feeling, not a formula. Each mood maps to a wave type, colour palette, animation speed, and line density. The same radial composition transforms entirely based on which wave drives it.

Wave Zoo

A bestiary. Each of the 34 formulas becomes a creature with a name, a temperament, and a living animated portrait. Hover to meet them — every wave has a personality, not just a shape.

concept: identical composition — only the formula changes

One Scene, 34 Moods

Same grid. Same colours. Same objects. Only the wave formula changes. Auto-cycles or click a number — the formula alone determines the atmosphere. The clearest proof that p5.waves is about character, not just shape.

concept: material simulation without physics

Fabric

Horizontal threads pinned at two points. No physics engine — just Waves.wave() per pixel. Switch the formula and the material changes: silk flows, burlap crinkles, rubber bounces, grass sways.

Particle Forces

Waves determine force direction and magnitude for hundreds of particles. The formula defines the system's personality — smoke drifts organically, confetti tumbles, fireflies pulse and wander, sparks scatter.
